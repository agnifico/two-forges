---
title: Ode to The Innocents
label: Tears of the Sun
intro: Should the Empire rise again, thou shalt be consecrated.
---
To my special person, to the kids in Filistin, Syria, Lebanon, Iran, and all across the world incuding my homeland India, to the ones who were lost, to the men who called it a day, and the women who never made it past the night, the ones who went down fighting, and the ones who couldn't - I will remember you.
