Conceptualised, designed and written by **Agnihotra Bhattacharya**. Coded with Claude in VS Code: SvelteKit and three.js.

Accessibility checked with Lighthouse and axe: WCAG 2.2 AA colour contrast by day and night, full keyboard access, screen-reader landmarks, and reduced motion respected.

## Type

Tanker, Author, Clash Grotesk and Switzer from Fontshare by the Indian Type Foundry. Chakra Petch by Cadson Demak. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next by the Braille Institute.

## Models and sound

3D models and sound effects by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) (CC0): Fantasy Town Kit, Pirate Kit, Survival Kit, Graveyard Kit, Platformer Kit, Castle Kit, Furniture Kit, Mini Arena and Mini Arcade; Impact Sounds, RPG Audio and UI Audio.

The War Forge's hero: ["Hero"](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hero-2e9bb3c86c38446d853f35808207058a) by [silvercrow101](https://sketchfab.com/silvercrow101), [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).

## Music

Themes from the JunesForge songbook, made with Gemini and edited by hand: Rain in the Solar City, Anklet of Stars and Crown of the Silent Fortress.
