title: Strife-born Sand Pine
label: The Rare Sand Pine
intro: A symbol of perseverance.
---
The sand pine is seen commonly across the land, but this rare mutation grows in the blood soaked Arena sand.

They say trees have memories. The red one has witnessed the empire rise, fall, and rise again. In that time the people have learnt that ==the Strife-born grows as people find it in them to defy fate, and simply persevere.==
