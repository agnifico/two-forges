---
title: The Seventh Floor
label: Higher than gods
intro: Seven floors of living things, then one floor with nothing but a bed and a mirror.
---
Oda Nobunaga raised Azuchi Castle over Lake Biwa, seven floors high. The story goes that every floor
paid tribute to living things, and rose as they did. The lowest was given to the small and gentle:
rabbits, songbirds, the little lives of the field. Above them came elephants, then tigers, then
dragons, then the supernatural, and on the sixth floor, the gods.

The seventh floor was Nobunaga's own. It held no painting, no carving, no statue of any living
thing. Only a bed, and a mirror.

The one image on that floor was the man who looked into it, the one he had placed above the gods.
