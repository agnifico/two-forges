From the handheld GameBoy based Pokemon games in pixel art style to Assassin's Creed set in ancient Greece, I've played a fair number of video games, and they have taught me a lot. 

**But it has also taught me a fair bit of history, geography, politics, science - the basics.** When games are built with care, and effort, they can educate young minds. 

So as a result, UX/UI, software dev, psychology, math, materials, revenue and profitability, intent, physics, cooking, stories, wars and revolutions...for me, all of them are simply games with different win/loss conditions.

==And I'm good at understanding games.==
